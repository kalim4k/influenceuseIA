# Décors

Bibliothèque de décors décrits **en 3 plans** (premier plan, plan moyen, arrière-plan), avec la lumière, la **vie en arrière-plan** (pour les vidéos et pour animer les photos) et le son. Les descriptions tirées des sources sont génériques : **remplace toujours les éléments par des équivalents réels de la ville de la fiche** (`pays.md`). Par exemple, une terrasse vue mer à Abidjan donne « a terrace overlooking the Ébrié lagoon and the Plateau skyline » ; à Dakar, « a terrace at Les Almadies overlooking the Atlantic ».

## Pourquoi 3 plans ?

Une image IA « fait IA » quand le décor est vide, flou ou trop parfait. Une vraie photo de téléphone a un **premier plan** (un objet coupé par le cadre), un **plan moyen** net (là où elle est) et un **arrière-plan** vivant et détaillé (des gens qui font leur vie, des voitures, de la végétation). En vidéo, ces figurants doivent bouger à leur propre rythme et ne jamais regarder la caméra.

## Intérieurs

**D1 Salle de bain en marbre (selfie miroir)** ★
```
FOREGROUND: the edge of a white marble vanity with a few skincare bottles, a folded white towel and a gold tap, partly cut by the frame.
MIDGROUND: her reflection in a large frameless mirror with a soft LED backlight, faint water spots and a light fog at the mirror's edges.
BACKGROUND: grey-veined marble walls, a frameless glass shower with a black rainfall showerhead and droplets on the glass, a small window letting in daylight.
LIGHT: warm LED mirror light on her face mixed with cool daylight from the window, soft shadows.
```
Son : léger écho de salle de bain, eau qui goutte, ventilation.

**D2 Chambre, lit défait (selfie au lit)** ★
```
FOREGROUND: rumpled white cotton duvet and the corner of a pillow very close to the lens.
MIDGROUND: her lying on her side on white pillows against a grey tufted upholstered headboard.
BACKGROUND: a white bedside table with a small linen lamp and a glass of water, a closed white door, a curtain half drawn.
LIGHT: soft morning daylight from a window on the side, gentle shadows on the sheets.
```

**D3 Salon de son appartement** (à garder identique d'un post à l'autre)
```
FOREGROUND: the arm of a beige bouclé sofa and a coffee table with a ceramic mug and an open magazine.
MIDGROUND: her standing near a large window, a tall monstera plant in a woven basket beside her.
BACKGROUND: light walls with two framed abstract prints, a floor lamp, sheer white curtains moving slightly, the city visible through the glass.
LIGHT: bright natural daylight from the window, warm and soft.
```
Vie : rideaux qui bougent, le chat de la fiche qui traverse.

**D4 Couloir de l'appartement ou hall (outfit check)**
```
FOREGROUND: nothing, a clean floor leading to her.
MIDGROUND: her standing in the middle of a narrow hallway with white walls and white doors with black handles.
BACKGROUND: light oak flooring, recessed ceiling spots, a wall mirror on the side, a window at the end of the corridor.
LIGHT: warm ceiling spots plus daylight from the end of the corridor.
```

**D5 Cuisine moderne**
```
FOREGROUND: a marble kitchen counter with a fruit bowl (mangoes, bananas, or the local fruit), a coffee machine edge.
MIDGROUND: her leaning on the counter.
BACKGROUND: flat-front wooden cabinets, a black fridge, pendant lights, a window with plants.
LIGHT: daylight from the window, warm pendant glow.
```

**D6 Ascenseur (révélation)**
```
MIDGROUND: brushed stainless-steel elevator doors in a hotel or residential building, the interior with a mirror wall and soft top light.
BACKGROUND (outside): a polished stone floor corridor, warm ceiling lights, a framed notice on the wall.
LIGHT: cool interior elevator light vs warm corridor light.
```

**D7 Voiture (selfie ceinture)**
```
FOREGROUND: the steering wheel edge and the dashboard (no logo), a phone mount.
MIDGROUND: her in the driver's seat with black leather seats, the seatbelt across her chest.
BACKGROUND: through the side windows, a parked street with storefronts and passersby, slightly overexposed daylight.
LIGHT: natural side light from the driver's window, soft shadows on her face.
```

## Ville

**D9 Quartier d'affaires**
```
FOREGROUND: the edge of a planter with tropical plants, a lamppost.
MIDGROUND: a wide clean sidewalk with light paving.
BACKGROUND: glass office towers reflecting the sky, palm trees, parked cars, a few people in office clothes walking with phones.
LIGHT: late-afternoon sun from the side, long shadows on the pavement, reflections on the glass.
```
Vie : gens qui marchent à des vitesses différentes, taxis et VTC qui passent, feuilles de palmier qui bougent.

**D10 Rue commerçante animée**
```
FOREGROUND: the corner of a café table or a street sign pole partly in frame.
MIDGROUND: a lively street with shop windows (no readable brand names), café chairs on the sidewalk.
BACKGROUND: shoppers carrying bags, a street vendor, scooters, string lights between buildings.
```

**D11 Terrasse de café ou de restaurant**
```
FOREGROUND: a small round table with an iced coffee, a croissant or a local snack, sunglasses on the table.
MIDGROUND: her seated on a woven bistro chair.
BACKGROUND: other tables with people chatting and eating, a waiter in a white shirt carrying a tray, white parasols, plants in pots.
```
Vie : le serveur passe, des clients rient, une serviette bouge avec le vent.

**D12 Rooftop au coucher du soleil**
```
FOREGROUND: the rim of a glass of fresh juice or a cocktail on a ledge.
MIDGROUND: her standing at a glass railing on a rooftop lounge with low sofas and cushions.
BACKGROUND: the city skyline at golden hour, a few guests in the lounge, a bartender, string lights just turning on.
LIGHT: warm golden backlight with her face lit from the side by the low sun.
```

**D13 Marché local** (fierté culturelle)
```
FOREGROUND: stacks of colorful fabrics or baskets of fruit and spices.
MIDGROUND: her walking between stalls.
BACKGROUND: vendors arranging goods, customers negotiating, umbrellas and tarps, motorbikes passing at the edge.
```
Règle : pas de vendeur qui fixe la caméra, ni de gros plan sur un inconnu.

**D14 Centre commercial**
```
MIDGROUND: a wide polished corridor of a modern mall, glass railings, shopfronts with no readable brand names.
BACKGROUND: shoppers on escalators, a fountain or atrium skylight, warm interior light.
```

## Événements

Chaque événement = **tenue de soirée ou de fête du style des références** (`garde-robe.md` § A et § K, jamais de tenue traditionnelle sauf demande) + **décor** + **figurants** cohérents. Les invités restent en arrière-plan, de dos ou de trois quarts, occupés.

**E1 Mariage traditionnel africain**
```
A traditional wedding celebration in a decorated hall or courtyard: rows of guests in elegant colorful party outfits, a flower-decorated stage with the couple seen from afar, gold chiavari chairs, drummers or a DJ booth, women dancing and spraying banknotes, warm string lights, gold and white drapes.
```
Elle est invitée (jamais la mariée, sauf demande explicite), au premier plan, rayonnante.

**E2 Mariage civil, réception élégante**
```
An elegant reception in a garden: round tables with white tablecloths and floral centerpieces, a cake table, guests chatting with glasses in hand, waiters carrying trays, fairy lights in the trees at dusk.
```

**E3 Gala, soirée de prestige**
```
A grand ballroom with crystal chandeliers, a red carpet and a photo wall without readable logos, guests in evening wear, photographers' flashes in the background.
```

**E4 Son anniversaire**
```
A private dinner table decorated with balloons in her palette colors, a tall layered cake with sparkler candles, friends (seen from behind or blurred) clapping and filming with their phones.
```

**E5 Fashion week ou défilé**
```
Outside a fashion venue: street-style photographers crouching with cameras, guests in avant-garde outfits, black cars dropping people off, metal barriers.
```

**E6 Concert ou festival**
```
A crowd with raised phones and hands, stage lights in purple and blue beams, confetti in the air, a big LED screen without readable text.
```

**E7 Lounge, boîte de nuit**
```
A dim upscale lounge: velvet booths, a bar with backlit bottles (no labels), neon in pink and blue, friends dancing in the background, light haze.
```
Règle : le visage reste bien éclairé (sinon le post performe mal).

**E8 Tabaski, Aïd, fête religieuse**
```
A family courtyard on celebration day: relatives in new colorful outfits, a table with festive dishes, children's laughter heard but children not shown in close-up, decorated home entrance.
```

**E9 Noël et Nouvel An**
```
A living room with a decorated Christmas tree and warm fairy lights, wrapped gifts, a window with city lights; for New Year: a rooftop with fireworks over the city and friends toasting.
```

**E10 Remise de diplôme**
```
A university courtyard with graduates in black gowns throwing caps, families taking photos, a stone or modern campus building.
```

**E11 Brunch entre amies**
```
A sunny brunch table with pancakes, fresh juices, avocado toast and local fruit; friends' hands reaching for food, laughter, plants and white tableware.
```

**E12 Fête nationale, carnaval, festival culturel**
```
A street parade with flags of the country, dancers in traditional costumes, drums, families watching from the sidewalk, confetti.
```
Utilise le vrai événement du pays (`pays.md` § Fêtes).
