## Comment faire

1. **Choisis un corps** dans la galerie et touche **Télécharger**.
2. Ouvre **GPT Image 2** (ChatGPT) ou **Nano Banana** (Gemini) et joins **2 images**, dans cet ordre :
   - **image 1** : une photo de ton influenceuse où son visage est bien net ;
   - **image 2** : le mannequin téléchargé.
3. **Copie le prompt** qui correspond à ton outil (plus bas), colle-le et envoie.
4. Génère 2 à 4 versions et **garde la meilleure** : ce sera la **référence du corps** de ton influenceuse. Joins-la avec son visage à chaque nouvelle génération.

Si le téléchargement ne démarre pas (navigateur d'Instagram, de WhatsApp…) : touche l'image pour l'ouvrir, puis appui long → **Enregistrer l'image**.

## Le prompt à copier

### GPT Image 2

```
REFERENCES: Image 1 = my AI influencer: her face, skin tone and hair. Keep her identity exactly. Image 2 = a black display mannequin, used ONLY as the reference for body shape, pose and framing.
TASK: Create a photorealistic photo of the woman from image 1 with exactly the body shape and proportions of the mannequin in image 2: same bust, waist and hip proportions, same seat, same thighs and legs, same height-to-width ratios. Copy the pose, the camera angle and the framing of image 2. If image 2 shows the mannequin several times, create the same number of views in the same layout, with the same woman in every view.
SUBJECT: an adult woman with the exact face of image 1: same face shape, eyes, nose, lips, eyebrows, hairline and hairstyle. Ignore the sculpted face and hair of the mannequin. Her real skin tone from image 1 covers her whole body with natural skin texture: nothing of the mannequin's black color or plastic shine remains.
OUTFIT: the same outfit and accessories as the mannequin, made of real fabric in realistic colors. If the mannequin wears nothing, she wears a fitted beige ribbed-knit long-sleeve midi dress.
SETTING: bright minimalist studio, plain white wall, light floor, soft daylight.
CAMERA & REALISM: real photo taken with a recent iPhone, sharp focus, natural skin with visible pores, light natural makeup, no filter, no CGI look, no text, no watermark.
FORMAT: same aspect ratio as image 2.
```

### Nano Banana

```
Using the first image as the exact reference for my AI influencer's face, skin tone and hair, and the second image (a black display mannequin) only as the reference for body shape, pose and framing, create a photorealistic photo of her. She is an adult woman with exactly the face of the first image and exactly the silhouette of the mannequin: the same bust, waist and hip proportions, the same seat, thighs and legs, in the same pose and from the same camera angle. If the second image shows the mannequin several times, create the same number of views in the same layout, with the same woman in every view.

Her natural skin tone from the first image covers her whole body with real skin texture; nothing of the mannequin's black color, plastic shine, sculpted face or sculpted hair remains. She wears the same outfit and accessories as the mannequin, in real fabric and realistic colors. If the mannequin wears nothing, she wears a fitted beige ribbed-knit long-sleeve midi dress.

The scene is a bright minimalist studio with a plain white wall and soft daylight. It looks like a real photo taken with a recent iPhone: sharp focus, natural skin with visible pores, light natural makeup, no filter, no text, no watermark. Same aspect ratio as the second image, high resolution.
```

## Astuces

- **Changer la tenue** : remplace la phrase de la tenue par celle que tu veux, par exemple `She wears a fitted emerald satin maxi dress.`
- **Changer le décor** : remplace la ligne du studio par un lieu, par exemple `SETTING: hotel rooftop terrace at sunset, city lights in the background.`
- **Le corps n'est pas assez fidèle** : ajoute `Match the mannequin's silhouette exactly, do not slim or soften the curves.`
- **Le visage change** : ajoute `Her face must be identical to image 1, like the same person photographed again.` et utilise une photo de face bien éclairée en image 1.
- **Refus de l'outil** : choisis un mannequin habillé, ou demande une tenue plus couvrante (robe longue, jean et top).
- **Ensuite** : joins cette photo comme référence du corps dans tous les prompts du skill, avec la photo du visage.
